## Trust relation

Journalists, researchers, and citizens cannot safely rely on an advertiser, submitter, or ordinary application server to preserve an exact ad artifact or select neutral sponsor evidence. A submitter can change the artifact URL, disclaimer, committee claim, candidate relation, or observation window; an application server could select favorable records or rewrite the result.

The contract freezes those inputs, independently fetches the exact artifact plus official OpenFEC committee and Schedule E evidence, and stores only a validator-agreed consequential projection. Unavailable, malformed, conflicting, weak-provenance, or model-inconsistent evidence cannot become a positive compatibility result. The on-chain consequence is an immutable assessment revision linked to the frozen trace.
